Summit Aviation Website Reflection

Where it started

Summit Aviation began with one idea in my mind: Create a place where pilots and aircraft owners could receive reliable aviation services in one location. Ben Ragsdell, the founder and CEO, developed a passion for aviation at a young age and had a desire to fly as well as to own a business. He worked to have his aviation business provide customers with a safe, convenient, and enjoyable experience. What started as a small business idea has grown into a full-service aviation facility providing aviation fuel, maintenance, aircraft rentals, and flight instruction. The goal from the beginning was to have a fully successful and self-sustaining aviation business.

Plans for growth

Like any company, Summit Aviation looks to the future to grow by expanding to many different locations, hoping to make a name for itself. The business wants to grow in all the services it provides. One area of growth includes expanding our maintenance bay. We would like to expand our physical locations as well as our capabilities. The business would like to grow to handle more maintenance tasks and support a wider variety of aircraft. Expanding Summit Aviation fleet of aircraft rentals for flight lessons and travel use would give more training opportunities and travel experiences. Summit Aviation wants to be known for being a convenient stop for pilots traveling through the area.

Who is involved

The people involved in Summit Aviation are key to the operation and success of the business, helping create an environment for pilots and aviation enthusiasts alike. Employees such as pilots, aircraft mechanics, flight instructors, customer service representatives, and other aviation professionals all play an important role in keeping the business running smoothly. As Summit Aviation grows, the team will continue to expand with skilled employees who bring experience, professionalism, and a positive attitude to the company.

Overall Reflection

Learning the process of website design has been a lot of work but extremely rewarding. Developing Summit Aviation has shown me how many different components there are to operate an aviation business successfully. Aviation is so much more than just planes and flying. Aviation involves so many working parts like maintenance, customer service, safety, management, and finances. Building Summit Aviation and the website has given me an opportunity to expand my skills and learn about website design. I want Summit Aviation to be a trustworthy destination.

"Reaching New Heights."
